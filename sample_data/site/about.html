About Meridian Mutual

A member-owned insurer for homes, cars, pets and trips, founded in Port Ellison in 1952.

Owned by the people we insure

Meridian Mutual began when a group of shipyard workers pooled their savings to rebuild a neighbor’s house after a storm. Seventy-four years later we insure 1.2 million households, but the idea is the same: members pay in, members are paid out, and there are no shareholders taking a cut.

That structure shapes how we work. We invest in claims handling rather than marketing, we price for the real risk of each home rather than for short-term growth, and we reward members who make their homes more resilient.
