Frequently asked questions

Does home insurance cover flood damage?

Not as standard. Rising water from rivers, storm surge or flash floods needs our optional flood cover. Escape of water inside the home, such as a burst pipe, is covered on every plan.

How is my rebuild value calculated?

Your rebuild value is what it would cost to rebuild your home from the ground up, not its market price. We estimate it from the size, age and construction of your home. Check it every year: building costs have risen 19% in three years.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Monthly payments cost nothing extra, or you can pay annually and save 5%.

How do I make a claim?

Call our 24/7 claims line on (800) 555-0199 or start a claim online. Members who report a claim within 24 hours are paid nine days sooner on average.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move between Basic, Standard and Premium at any time, and we recalculate your premium from the date of the change.
